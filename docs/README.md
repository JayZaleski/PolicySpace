# PolicySpace developer documentation

These documents explain how the code works, for developers who want to modify the project. The user manual (`manual.ipynb`) links here for technical detail.

| Document | Covers |
|---|---|
| [workspace.md](workspace.md) | The Cargo workspace and the Python package beside it, the crates and their dependency rule, the pinned toolchain, the Python environment, the check command, and test layout including the shared test helpers and recordings |
| [ids.md](ids.md) | The ID mechanism: `Id<K>`, `IdKind`, `NameRegistry<K>`, `IdVec<K, T>`, `NamedList<K, T>`, and `IdError` |
| [setting-loader.md](setting-loader.md) | The setting folder format, the loader's two phases, error reporting and the shared `ErrorList`, the section-type framework, `EntryReader` and its accessors and range checks, and the setting checker |
| [goods.md](goods.md) | What a good is, its two properties, how `[[good]]` sections are loaded, and how to add a property |
| [map.md](map.md) | Hex coordinates and orientation, `HexMap`, how `[[hex]]` sections are loaded, and how to add a hex attribute |
| [currency.md](currency.md) | Each hex's currency good: `HexCurrencies`, why it is per-hex state and not part of the map, and how the `currency` key of `[[hex]]` is read and resolved |
| [resources.md](resources.md) | Resource types and deposits, the `Quantity` type, deposit ownership and the per-owner index, how `[[resource]]` and `[[deposit]]` sections are loaded and their references checked |
| [market.md](market.md) | The dummy market: each hex's posted prices (`HexPrices`), the `Market` exchange and its `TradeError` refusals, and how `[[market]]` sections are loaded and checked |
| [pops.md](pops.md) | POP types (the endowment, the Stone-Geary utility parameters and how shares are normalized, and the growth parameters) and placed POPs (`Pops`, continuous size, one POP per type per hex), how `[[pop_type]]` and `[[pop]]` sections are loaded and checked, and the per-turn endowment |
| [technologies.md](technologies.md) | Technologies, the recipes factories run: inputs, output and capacity, the capital and construction bundles, construction turns and lifetime, what those numbers mean for depreciation, construction and maintenance, the shared `Bundle` type, and how `[[technology]]` sections are loaded and checked |
| [businesses.md](businesses.md) | Businesses, the named actors in the starting state: their hex, starting inventory and cash, the decider name, deposit ownership and how deposits are claimed, how `[[business]]` sections are loaded and checked, and the business stockpiles, answers and outcomes in the World |
| [factories.md](factories.md) | Factories, the assets that run a technology: their hex, technology, owning business and condition (the share of the capital bundle held), `Factories` with its per-hex and per-owner indexes, how `[[factory]]` sections are loaded and checked (including the owner's-hex check and the `count` helper shared with deposits), and the factory conditions written back each turn |
| [production.md](production.md) | Factory mechanics as pure functions over one business's factories, called once per business in the turn: the run and maintenance fractions, running at a fraction of capacity with shortages shared proportionally (which is how the cash cap reaches the run level), shutdown below one lifetime-slice, wear with exact factory lifetimes, maintenance and its caps, the run-wear-maintain order, and the per-factory records (`Operation`, `RunRecord`, `MaintenanceRecord`, `FactoryRecord`, `operate`, and the public slice rule `slices_held`) |
| [decider.md](decider.md) | The decider protocol as data: the `BusinessView` a business is served (built by `World::business_view`), the `Decision` it answers with (run and maintenance fractions per factory, offers, bids, a trace), validation against the view and its errors, the `Request`/`Reply`/`Recording` batch and reply validation, the lossless JSON format (name-keyed tables, quoted factory numbers, `Quantity` and `Turn` forms, two-layer reading, duplicate keys), one message per line as transport, and the committed recordings both test suites share and how to regenerate them |
| [python.md](python.md) | The Python package `policyspace`: setup in the virtual environment, the protocol client (dataclasses, strict reading, the engine-style writer and `format_float`), the decider registry, the line-per-turn loop and `python -m policyspace <decider>`, the arithmetic rule (margin test, orders per factory, offers and bids, the cash budget, the trace), and its tests |
| [decider-host.md](decider-host.md) | The decision point: the `DeciderHost` of named deciders the caller builds and passes to the World, name resolution, one batched request per decider per turn, the two-phase `Decider` trait, stored `BusinessAnswer`s and the report's counts and failures, the failure semantics, the external-process decider (time limit, grace window, pump threads, restart), the replay and closure deciders, and finding the project's Python (`host_for`, environment variables) |
| [demand.md](demand.md) | Stone-Geary demand: the pure `demand` function, the `PriceList` trait the market implements, tradable and untradable goods, the at-or-above and below-subsistence rules, and the `Demand` result the trade round and growth build on |
| [trade.md](trade.md) | The trade round in part 2 of the turn, for POPs and businesses: POP submissions and demand computed by the market, business submissions resolved from decisions, sales before purchases across a hex, the POP purchase cap and the business cash cap (one proportion for every bid), consumption, operating each business's factories right after its hex's round, leftover currency and slivers, and the per-POP `PopOutcome` and per-business `BusinessTrade` the World records |
| [growth.md](growth.md) | POP growth in part 2 of the turn: the subsistence index built from a POP's demand, the growth rate from its type's `growth` parameters (polynomial above subsistence, linear decline below, clamped), and how the World applies it to POP sizes |
| [world.md](world.md) | The running `World`, the three-part turn (emission and POP endowment; the decision point, the trade round with factory operation, and POP growth; perishing), hex, POP and business stockpiles with perish groups and removal, factory conditions written back each turn, per-POP trade outcomes, per-business answers and outcomes, turn reports with the total population, the business and factory totals and decider failures, and the setting checker's `--turns` option |
| [dev-ui.md](dev-ui.md) | The developer UI: its commands, modules, the `Session` that loads and steps settings under their deciders, the map math and the deposit, POP and business marks, the text it shows (turn lines, decider failure lines, panels, POP sections, business sections with their trade and factory tables, technology and business tables), the window, and the headless `check` mode |
